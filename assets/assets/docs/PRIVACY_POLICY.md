## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our pet-care ecosystem, including pet registration, adoption matching, mating matchmaker, NGO campaigns, and real-time community messaging.

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account & Profile Information:** When you register, we collect your phone number for OTP (One-Time Password) verification, your name, display nickname, city/location, and optional profile avatar photo.
* **Pet Information & Profiles:** Information about your pets, including pet name, species, breed, age, gender, temperament, health/vaccination logs, medical reminders, and photos.
* **Adoption & Mating Listings:** Information and photos you submit when creating pet adoption posts or mating/breeding profiles.
* **Direct Messages & Community Interactions:** Messages, inquiries, text, and context cards exchanged between users via our real-time messaging platform.
* **Customer Support:** Information you provide when contacting us for support, feedback, or inquiries.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Location Data:** With your explicit permission, we collect precise and approximate location data (GPS coordinates, city, and area) via your device's location services to enable nearby pet discovery, city-based adoption search, mating compatibility filters, and local pet care services.
* **Device & Log Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, network carrier, crash reports, and app performance logs.
* **Usage Data:** In-app actions, pages viewed, time spent on features, reminder interactions, and connection timestamps.

### C. Media & Camera Access
* With your explicit consent, we access your device’s **Camera** and **Photo Library / Storage** to allow you to capture and upload profile avatars, pet gallery images, adoption listings, and mating profile photos.

## 3. Device Permissions We Request

To provide key features of the TiflTails app, we may request the following permissions on your mobile device:

| Permission | Purpose | Optional / Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`)** | To detect your current area/city and display nearby pet adoption listings, compatible mating profiles, and local services. | Optional (You can manually select your city) |
| **Camera (`CAMERA`)** | To take new photos of your pet or profile picture directly within the app. | Optional (Requested on demand) |
| **Photos / Storage (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)** | To select existing photos from your gallery for pet profiles, avatars, and listings. | Optional (Requested on demand) |
| **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** | To deliver timely pet health reminders (vaccines, vet visits, medications) and new chat/inquiry notifications. | Optional |

You can enable or disable these permissions at any time through your device settings. Disabling certain permissions may limit specific app features.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Authentication & Account Management:** To create, verify, and maintain your user account securely via phone OTP and JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
2. **Community Discovery & Matching:** To connect pet owners for adoption opportunities, breeding/mating matchmaking, and NGO campaigns based on species, breed, and location.
3. **Real-Time Communication:** To deliver direct messages, inquiry cards, typing indicators, and message status updates between users.
4. **Health Tracking & Reminders:** To schedule and trigger local/in-app reminders for vaccinations, vet checkups, and pet care tasks.
5. **Safety, Trust & Security:** To monitor, detect, and prevent fraud, spam, abusive behavior, and illegal animal trade or exploitation.
6. **App Improvement & Analytics:** To troubleshoot issues, optimize performance, and improve user interface and experience.

## 5. How Your Information Is Shared

We respect your privacy and **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties. We only share information in the following situations:

* **Public Community Profiles:** Information you post to public listings (such as Pet Adoption or Mating listings, including pet photos, breed, age, and general city/area) is visible to other users of the App.
* **Direct User Communication:** When you message or send an inquiry to another user, your display name, avatar, and message content will be visible to that user.
* **Service Providers & Cloud Infrastructure:** We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., secure cloud hosting, S3 object storage for images, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim for reverse geocoding) who process data strictly on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
* **Legal & Safety Obligations:** We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, subpoena, or in good-faith belief to protect the rights, property, or safety of TiflTails users, animal welfare, or the public.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

* **Data Encryption:** All data transmitted between your device and our backend servers is encrypted using modern industry-standard protocols (**HTTPS / TLS and WSS**).
* **Secure Token Storage:** Authentication credentials and session tokens are encrypted and stored using secure device keystores (`FlutterSecureStorage`).
* **Access Control:** Server-side access to personal data and object storage buckets is strictly restricted to authorized systems with authenticated role-based access.
* **Data Hosting:** Images and media are stored in secured S3-compatible cloud storage buckets with access controls.

While we take all reasonable and standard precautions to safeguard your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

### Data Retention
We retain your personal data as long as your account remains active or as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements.

### Account and Data Deletion Request
In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, you have full control over your personal data:

1. **In-App Deletion:**
   * Navigate to **Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account**.
   * Confirming account deletion permanently removes your profile, pet registrations, active listings, and message history from active servers.

2. **Web / Email Request:**
   * If you are unable to access the app, you can submit a deletion request by emailing us at: **privacy@tifltails.com** or **support@tifltails.com** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"*.
   * Please provide the phone number associated with your account.
   * Upon verification, we will permanently delete or anonymize your personal data and associated records within **30 days**, unless retention is required by applicable law.
